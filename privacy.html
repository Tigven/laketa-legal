What we store

The e-mail address of your account: we use it to sign you in and to write to you about laketa. The data of a sign-in link is kept for 10 minutes.

Sessions: a parent's session lasts 12 hours, or 30 days on a trusted device; a child's session on a room link lasts 90 days.

Your room and its link.

Waitlist answers: e-mail address, the child's age band, consent to an interview, the campaign tag and the page you signed up on.

A planned call: your e-mail address, the day, time and time zone of the call, the child's age band if you gave it, the campaign tag, when you agreed to this policy, and when you opened the link from the calendar event or the e-mail. We use the address to send one e-mail with the calendar event and the call plan, and we may write once to ask how the call went. No newsletters. When the address has no room yet, the link from the event opens the new room without signing in until 48 hours after the planned time.

Page views: the page path and the campaign tag.

Landing page statistics, counted by Plausible Analytics on our own server in the USA: page, referrer and campaign, country, browser, operating system and device type, time on page, scroll depth and clicks on the sign-up button. Plausible sets no cookies, does not store IP addresses and does not link these statistics to an account.

Technical records of calls: times, which activities were used, connection quality, and the country each side joined from, derived from the IP address with a local database.

Session ratings: the 1–5 star rating you give a call after leaving the room, and your answer about what got in the way or what you liked.

Server logs, with IP addresses truncated.

Payments: Paddle customer and subscription ids, the plan, its status and period end, and the payment event records Paddle sends us about subscriptions. We never receive card data: Paddle holds payment details as Merchant of Record.

After a checkout, a pending-payment marker (the Paddle transaction id) is kept in your browser's local storage for up to 7 days.

What we do not do

Video and sound of calls are not recorded.

There is no child account and no child profile.

No ads and no third-party analytics.

Processors

Resend sends sign-in e-mails and the e-mail about a planned call.

Paddle processes payments as our Merchant of Record.

Cookies and local storage

Session cookies keep you signed in. Your browser's local storage keeps preferences and the pending-payment marker.

How long we keep data

Sessions and sign-in links expire on their own. Other data is kept while the account exists.

Your rights

To see, correct or delete your data, write to support@laketa.app. We answer within 30 days.

Children

A child joins only through the room link an adult shares. We do not ask children for personal data.

Changes

When what we store changes, we update this page and its effective date.
